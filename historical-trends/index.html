Learning Corner
Everything you need to know about reading and using this dashboard.
How to Read This Report
1
Select a market from the left panel. In Single Market mode, click any country to load its full data.
2
Choose a KPI from the pill bar at the top of the chart area. Time-series KPIs (GDP Growth, Inflation) show 2018–2025 trend lines.
3
Switch to Multi-Country mode to compare up to all 42 markets simultaneously. Use region shortcuts (+ APAC, + LATAM…) to add groups quickly.
4
Use Table View (Chart View toggle) to see a complete time-series table with color-coded cells, plus a CSV export button.
5
Trajectory mode: select two variables (X and Y) to plot economic cycles as a phase-space scatter — reveals stagflation, boom-bust, and recovery paths.
6
Correlation Explorer: scroll below the main chart area to compare two countries' trajectories side by side, with Pearson correlation automatically calculated.
7
The red dashed line on time-series charts marks 2020 (COVID-19 disruption year) for visual reference.
8
The FMCG Signal badge (Growing / Stable / Pressured / Volatile) is a composite score based on GDP growth, inflation, and demand stability.
KPI Definitions
GDP Growth Rate % yoy
Annual percentage change in real gross domestic product, measuring economic expansion or contraction.
Source: IMF WEO Oct 2025 / Jan 2026 Update
Higher = stronger economic growth and consumer demand base.
Inflation Rate % avg
Average annual consumer price index change. High inflation erodes purchasing power and compresses real FMCG volume.
Source: IMF WEO Oct 2025
2–4% is generally considered healthy. Above 6% = consumer stress.
Unemployment %
Share of the labor force actively seeking employment. High unemployment depresses household income and FMCG basket size.
Source: IMF WEO / ILO modeled estimates 2025
Lower is better for consumer spending capacity.
GDP per Capita PPP USD
GDP divided by population, adjusted for purchasing power parity. Best single indicator of consumer purchasing power.
Source: IMF WEO 2025 (intl. dollars)
Higher = greater affordability of branded FMCG products.
Urbanization %
Share of population living in urban areas. Urban consumers typically have higher FMCG spending and modern trade access.
Source: UN World Urbanization Prospects 2025
Higher urbanization generally correlates with modern retail penetration.
E-Commerce % % of retail
Online sales as a percentage of total retail. Critical indicator for digital channel investment decisions.
Source: Netguru / ClickPost / MobiLoud 2024–2025
China (47%) and South Korea (30%) lead; most EMs below 15%.
Internet Penetration %
Share of population with internet access. Proxy for digital readiness and omnichannel retail potential.
Source: ITU 2024
Markets above 85% are considered digitally mature.
Mobile per 100 subscriptions
Mobile subscriptions per 100 inhabitants. Values above 100 indicate multiple SIMs per person.
Source: ITU / GSMA 2024
High mobile penetration enables m-commerce and mobile payment ecosystems.
Gini Coefficient index
Measure of income inequality (0 = perfect equality, 100 = extreme inequality). High Gini signals bifurcated consumer markets.
Source: World Bank latest available
High Gini = premium and value tiers both grow, mid-tier squeezed.
Trade Openness % GDP
(Exports + Imports) / GDP. Higher values reflect greater integration with global supply chains and import access.
Source: World Bank WDI 2024
Very open markets (>100%) like Singapore, UAE are trade hubs.
FDI Inflows % GDP
Foreign direct investment as a percentage of GDP. Signal of business confidence and retail/FMCG international expansion activity.
Source: UNCTAD 2024
Higher FDI often precedes modern trade format expansion.
Private Consumption % GDP
Household final consumption expenditure as % of GDP. Measures how consumer-driven the economy is.
Source: World Bank WDI 2024
Guatemala (82%) and Pakistan (83%) are highly consumption-driven; Singapore (36%) is investment/trade-driven.
Govt Debt % GDP
General government gross debt as % of GDP. High debt limits fiscal stimulus capacity and may signal currency/inflation risk.
Source: IMF Fiscal Monitor 2025
Japan (254%), Italy (139%) are high-debt markets with constrained fiscal room.
Big Mac Index USD
Local price of a McDonald's Big Mac converted to USD. Informal proxy for purchasing power parity and currency valuation.
Source: The Economist, January 2025
Switzerland ($7.99) = most expensive. Russia ($2.27) = most undervalued vs USD.
Median Age years
The age that divides the population in half. Younger populations drive volume growth; older populations shift to health/convenience.
Source: UN WPP 2024 revision
Nigeria (18), Kenya (20) = youngest. Japan (49), Italy (47) = oldest.
Methodology & Sources
Data Vintage PRIMARY
IMF World Economic Outlook October 2025 + January 2026 Update. All GDP growth and inflation figures are IMF estimates unless noted.
Sources
  • IMF WEO Oct 2025 & Jan 2026 — GDP, inflation, current account
  • IMF Fiscal Monitor 2025 — Government debt
  • World Bank WDI 2024 — Trade openness, private consumption, Gini
  • UN World Urbanization Prospects 2025 — Urbanization
  • UN World Population Prospects 2024 — Population, median age
  • ILO World Employment & Social Outlook 2025 — Unemployment
  • The Economist Big Mac Index, January 2025
  • ITU / GSMA 2024 — Internet penetration, mobile subscriptions
  • UNCTAD 2024 — FDI inflows
  • Numbeo 2025 — Coke price (Coca-Cola 2L, supermarket)
  • Netguru / ClickPost / MobiLoud 2024–2025 — E-commerce %
Historical Data
GDP growth and inflation: 2018–2025 from IMF WEO database and World Bank. Some 2023–2024 values filled from IMF training data where web fetch was blocked.
FMCG Signal Methodology
Growing: real GDP >3% + inflation <6% + stable demand drivers
Stable: GDP 1–3%, contained inflation
Pressured: low/negative GDP or elevated inflation constraining spending
Volatile: high inflation, currency instability, or sharp GDP swings
Markets Coverage
42 markets across 5 regions: Americas, LATAM, Western Europe (WE), Eastern Europe / Middle East / Africa (EEMEA), Asia-Pacific (APAC). Data as of compile date 2026-05-14.
Update Frequency
Manually updated following each IMF WEO release (typically April and October). Extended KPIs updated annually.
